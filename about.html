HOLLOW KEEPa descent in four parts

The dark remembers every step.

HOLLOW KEEP is a free first-person medieval dungeon horror game that runs entirely in your web browser. You go down into a torchlit keep with a rusty sword, one flask, and a candle shrine that remembers you — and you keep descending until the thing on the throne is dead.

▶ Play HOLLOW KEEP Source on GitHub

What is in the game

  • Four descending levels — the Forgotten Cellars, the Ossuary Warrens, the Drowned Chapel, and the Hollow Throne — each with its own palette, texture set, score and enemies.
  • A three-phase boss: shield combos, braziers that go out and raise skeletons, enraged lunges and a radial shockwave you must jump or outrun.
  • Four weapons — sword, iron mace, spear and crossbow — with different reach, speed, stagger and a working parry window.
  • Torchlight survival: your torch burns down and must be refuelled with oil, while light is the only thing between you and the dark.
  • Keys, doors, gates and shortcuts that unlock from the far side, plus hidden caches and lore notes.
  • Spike traps, ceiling drips, floating dust and a dread director that whispers, slams doors and shows you things at the edge of the light.
  • Pixel-art PBR rendering: real normal maps and roughness on every surface, rendered at low resolution with dithering, vertex snapping, fog and torch shadows.

Death is a checkpoint, not a sentence: you retry at the last candle shrine with your inventory, your keys and your opened doors intact. Use headphones — the score and the drips are spatial.

Requirements

A desktop browser with WebGL 2 and a mouse and keyboard — Chrome, Edge or Firefox. It is a single page with no install, no account and no download beyond the game itself, and it works offline once cached.

About this project

HOLLOW KEEP is built with Three.js and Vite. Geometry, enemies, weapons and UI are generated in code; surface textures and a set of props are real CC0 scans from Poly Haven. All sound is synthesised in the browser with the Web Audio API — there are no audio files.
